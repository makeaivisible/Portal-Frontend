Final MVP direction

The final MVP should connect to major LLM platforms directly. Contributors will authorize connectors once, then eligible chats can sync in the background and results can be viewed on the website. Manual uploads are a fallback for early testing, not the intended long-term experience.

Before any sync or upload

  • Connectors should be opt-in, revocable, and clear about what is collected.
  • Manual uploads should use export files, not screenshots or passwords.
  • Remove anything you already know is private before upload or sync.
  • Raw conversation text is meant for anonymization, not publication.
  • Public results should be aggregate and privacy-protected.
